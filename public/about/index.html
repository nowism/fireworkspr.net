Amy Burke Bessette

Strategic Communications Advisor
Former Apple Communications Executive

Amy Burke Bessette is a seasoned communications executive and strategic advisor with nearly 30 years of experience building brands, shaping corporate narratives and guiding companies through high-profile business moments.

As a former Senior Director of Corporate Public Relations at Apple, Amy brings deep expertise in corporate reputation, media relations, executive communications, crisis management and brand positioning. Throughout her career, she has helped organizations navigate complex communications challenges, launch innovative products and initiatives, and develop strategies that strengthen reputation and drive business objectives.

Today, Amy leads an independent strategic communications consultancy, partnering directly with CEOs, founders and executive leadership teams to elevate their companies and strengthen their market positions. Her work spans corporate communications strategy, executive visibility, thought leadership, messaging development, media relations and fractional communications leadership.

With a background in both journalism and public relations, Amy combines strong editorial instincts with strategic business acumen. Known for her sound judgment, creativity and ability to operate in fast-paced, high-stakes environments, she brings a hands-on, collaborative approach to every engagement.

The most effective communications strategies begin with a deep understanding of the business. Great storytelling, trusted relationships and thoughtful execution create meaningful, lasting impact.
